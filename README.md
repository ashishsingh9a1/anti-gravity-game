# 🚀 ANTI-GRAVITY // OVERDRIVE

> **A fast-paced, neon-cyberpunk rogue-lite arena shooter built directly for the browser.**  
> Harness gravitational forces, bullet deflection, and quantum augmentations to obliterate the relentless swarm!

---

## 🕹 Quick Start & How to Play

### 1. Launch the Game
- Simply **double-click** `start_game.bat`, or
- Double-click **`index.html`** in your browser, or
- Run a local server: `python -m http.server 8000` and visit `http://localhost:8000`.

*No installation, external dependencies, or build tools required! Works 100% offline.*

## ⚡ Core Anti-Gravity Mechanics

1. **Anti-Gravity Pulse (Space / Right-Click)**:
   - **Bullet Reflector**: Deflects all hostile projectiles in a 250px radius, flipping their polarity into high-damage homing missiles targeting enemies!
   - **Swarm Repulsion**: Blasts colliding enemies backward with gravitational shockwaves.
   - **Crystal Vacuum**: Instantly sucks every floating XP crystal on screen straight to your ship.
2. **Spacetime Grid Warping**:
   - The retro-vector spacetime mesh bends and warps dynamically around singularities, thrusters, and shockwave explosions.
3. **Phase Dash (Shift)**:
   - Grants temporary invulnerability frames (i-frames) and high-speed momentum through bullet curtains.
4. **Kill Combos**:
   - String together kills within 3.2 seconds to ramp your score multiplier from **1.0x up to 10.0x**!

## 🛸 Playable Ships

### 1. Apex Striker (Balanced Assault)
- **Trait**: Extended Pulse Shockwave radius, +25% Critical Hit Chance, rapid pulse recharge.
- **Playstyle**: Versatile aggressive brawler with high burst damage.

### 2. Vortex Spectre (Graviton Anomaly)
- **Trait**: Unlocks **Singularity Well** right from Wave 1, +20% Flight Speed, reduced Dash cooldown.
- **Playstyle**: High-mobility hit-and-run pilot manipulating miniature black holes.

### 3. Aegis Bastion (Heavy Dreadnought)
- **Trait**: Starts with **Dual Orbital Plasma Moons**, +30% Max Hull and Energy Shields.
- **Playstyle**: Immovable tank with passive orbiting shields that slice through swarms.

## 🔮 Rogue-lite Upgrade Arsenal

Every time your ship collects enough Grav-Crystals to level up, choose between 3 randomized holographic upgrade cards:

- **Multi-Plasma Array**: Adds extra simultaneous plasma cannons.
- **Graviton Railgun**: Fires relativistic beams that pierce infinitely through lines of enemies.
- **Seeker Swarm**: Launches homing micro-missiles that curve around obstacles.
- **Singularity Well**: Drops miniature black holes that pull in hostile swarms before collapsing in a supernova.
- **Tesla Arc Conductor**: High-voltage chain lightning that jumps between targets.
- **Plasma Orbitals**: Deploys orbiting satellites that slice through enemies on contact.
- **Grav-Pulse Amplifier**: Expands the radius and decreases cooldown of your Anti-Gravity Pulse.
- **Kinetic Converter**: Reflected enemy bullets deal +150% additional damage.
- **Aegis Barrier & Nano-Repair Matrix**: Max shield, shield regeneration, and hull repairs.

## 👾 Enemy Ranks & Bosses

- **Swarmers**: Fast triangular drones that hunt in packs.
- **Orbiters**: Circle at fixed gravitational orbits while firing concentrated plasma bursts.
- **Kamikaze Dashers**: Lock on with targeting lasers before rocketing across the screen at hyper-velocity.
- **Grav-Weavers**: Heavy octagonal dreadnoughts that cast tractor beams on your ship.
- **BOSS: Leviathan Dreadnought (Wave 5 / 10)**:
  - Massive multi-stage boss featuring bullet hell spirals, gravitational collapse attacks, and laser sweeps!

## 🎧 Procedural Web Audio Engine

- **Zero audio assets to download** — all music and sound effects are synthesized in real-time using the browser's Web Audio API (`AudioContext`, oscillators, custom biquad filters, and noise buffers).
- **Adaptive Synthwave Soundtrack**: Dynamic 16th-note rolling bassline, punchy 80s kick/snare, and high arpeggios that automatically ramp in intensity as waves progress.
